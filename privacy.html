Stream Predictor – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-10-12

What we collect

  • Twitch Extension token claims: channel_id, user_id, role.
  • Basic server logs (time, IP, user-agent) for security/diagnostics.

We do not use Identity Linking (ID Share). We do not use third-party analytics or tracking cookies.

How we use the data

Authorize access, associate participation and scores to your Twitch account ID, prevent abuse, and operate the Extension.

Sharing

No selling of data. Operational providers (e.g., Cloudflare) may process data strictly to deliver the service.

Retention

Kept only as long as necessary for operation and security, then deleted or anonymized.

Security

HTTPS in transit; limited access.
